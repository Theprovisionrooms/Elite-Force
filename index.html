Elite ForceHow we work

How an engagement works

The same careful sequence whether you need a protection team next week or want to recover a debt that has been outstanding for years.

  1. Private consultation

    A confidential conversation with a director, under a non-disclosure agreement if you prefer.

  2. Assessment

    We review the risk, the paperwork or the debt, and tell you plainly what is achievable.

  3. Proposal

    A written plan with a fixed scope, team, timescale and cost. No surprises later.

  4. Deployment

    Operatives briefed and in place, or recovery action under way, on the agreed date.

  5. Reporting

    Regular updates from one point of contact until the job is complete.

Elite ForceQuestions

Questions clients ask

Who does Elite Force work with?

High net worth individuals, families, family offices, business owners and companies across the UK who need private security, close protection or the recovery of significant sums owed to them.

Do you cover the whole of the UK?

Yes. We operate across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, including London, Manchester, Cheshire, Birmingham, Leeds, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Cardiff and Belfast.

How quickly can close protection be arranged?

It depends on the size of the team and the level of risk, but short-notice requests are assessed on the day they are received. Planned engagements are best discussed as early as possible so the right operatives and routes can be prepared.

How does your debt recovery work?

Every case starts with a review of the debt and the paperwork behind it, followed by tracing where needed, direct negotiation with the debtor and, if required, support through the formal legal and enforcement process. All recovery is carried out lawfully and professionally.

Is my enquiry kept confidential?

Yes. Enquiries are read only by a director, and a confidentiality agreement can be signed before any detail of your situation is shared.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is priced to the requirement. Security is quoted on the team, duration and risk level. Debt recovery can be arranged on a fixed fee or a percentage of the sum recovered, agreed in writing before work begins.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes. Message +44 7348 131285 on WhatsApp and a director will pick up the conversation. You can also call the same number or email enquiries@eliteforce.site.
