The Elite Force Private Client Suite

Three digital services that turn one-off security and recovery jobs into monthly retainers. Designed, built and run by Sidedoor Digital, branded entirely as Elite Force.

The idea

Wealthy clients do not just want a team to turn up. They want to see that they are protected. At the moment most security firms send a PDF once a week and a debt update when someone chases it.

The Private Client Suite gives every Elite Force client something to open on their phone: their threat level, where their team is, how much of their money has been recovered and a direct, encrypted line to their account director.

That visibility is what justifies a monthly fee. It is also what makes clients stay, because once their family, properties and cases live inside Elite Force, moving to another firm means starting again.

Private client portal

A members-only app and web portal where clients see their live threat assessment, track debt recovery and request help in an emergency. Dark, simple and fast, with nothing to learn.

  • Face ID and fingerprint sign-in using passkeys, so there are no passwords to steal or forget.
  • Zero-knowledge storage. Case notes and documents are encrypted on the client's device before upload, so not even the hosting provider can read them.
  • Live recovery tracking for every case: amount owed, amount recovered and the next action.
  • Emergency extraction request with a press-and-hold button that alerts the duty team with the client's location.
  • Installs like an app on iPhone and Android, with no App Store approval needed.

Encrypted messaging

A private, Elite Force branded messenger for clients, their families and the operations team. No personal numbers, no WhatsApp groups, nothing on anyone else's servers.

  • End-to-end encrypted messages, calls and file sharing, built on the open Matrix standard used by governments and defence organisations.
  • Hosted in the UK on Elite Force's own server, so the company controls every message and every account.
  • Disappearing messages and remote sign-out if a phone is lost or a staff member leaves.
  • Optional hardened handsets for top-tier clients, supplied through a specialist partner and set up before they are handed over.

Live threat dashboard

Instead of a weekly PDF, top clients get a live, interactive map of everything they own and everything they are owed. Properties, vessels and holdings, known risk areas, and the real-time status of every debt Elite Force is recovering for them.

  • Every asset on one map, with its security status and the last time it was checked.
  • Risk areas updated by the Elite Force team, with travel advice attached.
  • Debt portfolio view showing the total owed, total recovered and each case's stage.

How it would be delivered

  1. Weeks 1 to 4. Client portal with passkey sign-in and recovery tracking, tested with two or three existing clients.
  2. Weeks 5 to 7. Encrypted messaging set up on a UK server, staff onboarded, first clients invited.
  3. Weeks 8 to 11. Live threat dashboard, admin screen for the team, then the tiers go on sale.

One portal subscription covers the build within months

Two Sovereign clients pay for the full suite in under two months. Everything after that is margin, and every client inside the portal is a client who is far less likely to leave.
